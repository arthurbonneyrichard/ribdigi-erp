| Pack | Scope |
|------|--------|

- Distinct from Stage 171 K1 packaging, Stage 191 hosted FAQ SaaS remaining-gate, and Stage 214 support runbook remaining-gate.
- Honesty flags stay false.
- Stages 1–214 feature scopes remain frozen.
